# Front-End Engineering Interview Masterclass — The Book

A book-form companion to Interview Kickstart's [Front-End Engineering Interview Masterclass](https://learn.interviewkickstart.com/course/front-end-engineering-interview-masterclass#curriculum), written for the same target reader as the ML book in the parent folder: a frontend engineer with 8+ years of experience in a data-center role, aiming for senior FE roles at FAANG.

## What's in this book vs the ML book

The IK Front-End and ML Masterclass curricula share **Section I (DSA)** and **Section II (System Design)** verbatim — same modules, same case studies, same expectations. To avoid duplicate writing, those chapters live in the ML book and are referenced here.

The unique content of the Front-End curriculum is **Section III: Front-End Domain Course** (JavaScript, UI/DOM, frontend system design, patterns/tools/techniques). This is the heart of this book.

## Voice and conventions

Same as the ML book — direct, opinionated, anchored in production systems, target reader is a frontend engineer pivoting toward senior FE roles at FAANG. Each chapter ends with **The Interview Move** — the 90-second answer that earns senior signal.

The FE-specific content references real production frontend systems (Netflix's video player, Facebook's News Feed, Instagram's infinite scroller, Bootstrap's component model) and the kinds of decisions a staff FE engineer at one of those companies actually makes.

Reading time for the FE-specific chapters: ~2 hours. Reading the full curriculum (including the ML book's shared sections): ~6 hours.
